About us

A new, locally owned company

Fleetrex Leasing LLC is a new, locally owned truck leasing company based in Charlotte, North Carolina. We lease semi tractors to carriers and logistics companies.

We focus on two things: personal service and reliable equipment. When you call, you talk directly to the owners, not a call center. We take time to understand how you operate and agree on a lease that works for both sides.
