How it works

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Call, email or send the form below with a few details about your company and what you haul.

  2. 2

    Discuss your needs

    We talk through the tractor you need, how long you need it and how you plan to run it.

  3. 3

Flexible terms

Every lease is tailored to you

No two carriers run the same way, so we don't use a one-size-fits-all contract. Each lease is agreed individually with the carrier.

Before signing, we discuss and agree on the details that matter to your business, including:

  • Lease length
  • Insurance
  • Fuel
  • Maintenance and repairs
  • Payment schedule

About us

A new, owner-operated company

Fleetrex Leasing LLC is a new, owner-operated truck leasing company based in Charlotte, North Carolina. We lease semi tractors to carriers and logistics companies.

We focus on two things: personal service and reliable equipment. When you call, you talk directly to the owner, not a call center. We take time to understand how you operate and agree on a lease that works for both sides.
